## Connect the ad to the destination

Advertising should not exist in isolation. The page a person reaches, the message they see and the way an enquiry is captured should make sense as one journey.

## Use the right channel for the goal

Google Ads can help reach people actively searching for products and services. Meta Ads can support awareness, leads and conversions across Facebook and Instagram.

## Keep the follow-up practical

Lead forms, WhatsApp and other communication paths can help a business respond to enquiries.
