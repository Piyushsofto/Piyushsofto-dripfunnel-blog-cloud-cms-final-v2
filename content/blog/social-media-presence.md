## Stay visible where customers spend time

Social media can help a business communicate regularly, show what it offers and stay visible to potential customers.

## Do not make social media your only foundation

A website gives the business a dedicated digital destination it can use to explain services, capture enquiries and connect visitors to the next step.

## Connect visibility to action

Social content becomes more useful when it points people toward a clear action, such as visiting a website, starting a WhatsApp conversation or submitting an enquiry.
