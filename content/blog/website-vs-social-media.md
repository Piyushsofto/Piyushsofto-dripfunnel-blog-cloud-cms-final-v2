## Reach and destination serve different purposes

Social media is useful for reaching and communicating with audiences. A website gives people a dedicated place to understand the business, explore services or products and take the next action.

## Create a connected journey

A business can use social content and advertising to attract attention, then use its website and lead-capture tools to turn that attention into enquiries or purchases.

## Build a system rather than choosing one

The strongest digital presence is a practical combination that helps the business attract, capture, convert and grow.
